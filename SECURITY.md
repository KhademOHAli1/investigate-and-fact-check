# Security reports

Report a vulnerability privately through [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/KhademOHAli1/investigate-and-fact-check/security/advisories/new). Do not include confidential sources, private evidence, or working credentials. Provide a minimal synthetic reproduction, the affected release, and the observed impact.

Version 1.x is the current maintenance line. There is no guaranteed response-time or commercial support agreement.

The manifest helper is intended for stable directories under your control. It does not provide immutable custody or a hardened boundary against a concurrently hostile filesystem. Review skill instructions and scripts before installation; a skill operates with its host's available permissions.
